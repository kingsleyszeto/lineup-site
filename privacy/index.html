Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 September 2026

Lineup is a personal app for keeping track of concerts you're going to and seeing what your friends are going to. This policy describes exactly what it stores and who it is shared with. It is short because Lineup collects very little.

The short version

  • Lineup has no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.
  • It doesn’t ask for your location, contacts, or microphone.
  • Your data is not sold, rented, or shared for advertising. Ever.
  • You can delete your account, and everything in it, from inside the app.

What Lineup stores

Your account

Lineup uses Sign in with Apple as its only sign-in method. When you sign in, Apple gives us an email address and an identifier for your account. If you choose Hide My Email, Apple gives us a private relay address instead of your real one, and we never see your real address. Either works fine.

Lineup never receives, and never asks for, your Apple ID password.

Your profile

The username and display name you choose when you first sign in, and a short bio if you add one. Your username and display name are visible to people you are friends with.

What you track

The shows you mark yourself as going to or interested in, how many tickets you hold, and any star ratings and written notes you leave about a show or a venue. Your notes are yours; they are not shown to anyone else.

Your friends

Who you are friends with, and the invite links you create or accept. Friendship in Lineup only happens when someone sends you an invite link and you open it — there is no public directory and nobody can add you without one.

What’s planned

Profile photos are coming. Lineup does not ask for access to your photos today and stores none of them, so for now there is nothing here to collect. When that changes, iOS will ask your permission first, and this policy — and the date at the top of it — will be updated to describe exactly what is stored before the feature reaches you.

Who else is involved

Three services, and no others:

  • Apple — handles signing you in, through Sign in with Apple.
  • Supabase — hosts the database and accounts. This is where your data physically lives. Supabase processes it on our behalf and does not use it for anything else.
  • Ticketmaster — when you search for a show, the words you typed are sent to Ticketmaster's public event API to find matching events. Your identity is not sent with it — Ticketmaster receives the search text, not who searched.

Lineup contains no third-party analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Deleting your account

Open Settings (the gear icon on the You tab) and choose Delete account.

This permanently deletes your account, your profile, the shows you tracked, your ratings and notes, and your friend connections. It also revokes Lineup's Sign in with Apple credential, so Lineup disappears from your Apple ID settings. It cannot be undone, and there is no grace period or hidden copy.

Shows themselves are not deleted, because other people may be attending them — but they are no longer linked to you in any way.

How long data is kept

For as long as you have an account. When you delete your account it is removed immediately, other than short-lived operational backups which age out on their own.

Your rights

You can see everything Lineup holds about you from inside the app, change your profile at any time in Settings, and delete all of it whenever you want. If you'd like a copy of your data in another form, or have a question about any of this, email lineup@kingsleyszeto.me.

Depending on where you live you may have additional rights over your data, including access, correction and erasure. The app already gives you all three directly; email the address above for anything it doesn't cover.

Children

Lineup is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what is collected or who it is shared with, the date at the top will change and the app will say so before the change applies to you.
